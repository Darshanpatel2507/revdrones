WHAT IT DOES

Built for powerline inspection data.

01

Insulator & hardware defect detection

Cracked, flashed-over, or damaged insulators and hardware are flagged with the tower and span identified.

02

Conductor damage flagging

One consolidated report covering the full inspected span, tower by tower.
