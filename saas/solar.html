Revolutionized Solar

Thermal fault detection for solar farms. Thermal and RGB imagery from your solar farm inspection flights gets processed automatically — hotspot cells, string failures, and soiling patterns identified and mapped back to the exact panel location, not just a general area of the array.

In development — early access opening soon
WHAT IT DOES

Built for solar inspection data.

01

Hotspot & string failure detection

Thermal anomalies are automatically matched against expected panel temperature profiles to flag failing cells and strings.

02

Soiling & vegetation overlay tracking

Dust buildup and vegetation encroachment are tracked over time, so cleaning schedules are based on data, not guesswork.

03

Panel-level fault mapping

Every finding is pinned to a specific panel on your site layout, not a vague coordinate.

04

Automated inspection reporting

Consistent, exportable reports after every flight, ready to share with asset owners.
