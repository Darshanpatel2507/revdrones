Industries We Serve

Mining sites are vast, hazardous, and constantly changing. Aerial mapping and monitoring give you a current, accurate picture of the site without putting people in harm's way.

HOW WE HELP

What we do for Mining.

01

3-D Mapping

Detailed 3-D maps of mining sites, capturing topographic data and providing accurate, high-resolution models of terrain and infrastructure — essential for planning and operational decision-making.

02

Blast Monitoring and Analysis

Our drones monitor blasting operations, capturing data on blast patterns and impacts. This information is analyzed to optimize future activities, improving efficiency and safety while minimizing explosive use.

03

Security Surveillance

Real-time security surveillance of mining sites, monitoring for unauthorized access, theft, and other security threats — comprehensive aerial coverage of large areas, issues detected and addressed quickly.
