HOW WE HELP

What we do for Agriculture.

01

Crop Monitoring

Our drone-based crop monitoring service provides real-time aerial imaging and data collection, allowing farmers to observe crop conditions, growth stages, and identify areas that require attention.

02

Crop Health Analysis

Unlock the secrets of your fields with advanced crop health analysis. Using multispectral and thermal imaging, we assess plant health, chlorophyll levels, and potential disease threats — giving you invaluable insight into your crops' vigor all season long.

03

Pesticide Spraying

Say goodbye to uneven pesticide application with precise drone-based spraying. Equipped with cutting-edge spraying systems, our drones ensure uniform distribution, covering vast areas swiftly and efficiently.

04

Farm Survey

Transform your agricultural planning with comprehensive farm survey methods. High-resolution drone imagery creates detailed 2D and 3D maps of your terrain, including soil analysis and optimized land use planning.

05

Seed Dropping

Perfect for reforestation projects, cover cropping, and challenging terrains — our drones evenly distribute seeds over large areas, reaching every corner of your land, even the hard-to-access spots.
