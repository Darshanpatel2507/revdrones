IND / RE — RENEWABLE ENERGY

Efficient renewable energy maintenance with drones.

Industries We Serve

Solar farms and wind installations are large, hard to inspect, and costly to shut down. Aerial thermal and structural inspection catches issues early — without scaffolding, downtime, or risk to your maintenance crew.

HOW WE HELP

What we do for Renewable Energy.

01

Solar Farm Inspection

High-resolution and thermal imaging inspections of solar farms let us spot defects, dirt buildup, and performance issues with precision, ensuring your solar farm operates at maximum efficiency.

02

Cleaning Services

Automated drone cleaning keeps both solar panels and windmills free of dust, dirt, and debris — maximizing the efficiency and longevity of your renewable energy systems.

03

Structural Inspection

Detailed structural inspections of wind turbines and solar panel mounts using high-resolution imaging and thermal data, identifying structural issues early and extending infrastructure lifespan.
