HOW WE HELP

What we do for Academics.

01

Lab Setup

We assist educational institutions in setting up advanced drone labs, providing the necessary equipment, software, and training that supports hands-on learning and research in drone technology.

02

Workshops

Workshops on drone technology covering operation, maintenance, and applications across various industries — valuable knowledge and skills for students and professionals alike.

03

RPTO — Remote Pilot Training Organization

We collaborate with institutions to establish RPTO certification programs, developing curriculum, providing training materials, and assisting with regulatory compliance so students become certified drone pilots.
