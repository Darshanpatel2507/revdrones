HOW WE HELP

What we do for Construction & Infrastructure.

01

Land Survey

Detailed land surveys capture accurate topographic data and create comprehensive maps for construction planning and development, identifying potential challenges and opportunities before construction begins.

02

Aerial Mapping

High-resolution aerial maps built with advanced drone technology offer a comprehensive view of construction sites, assisting in planning, monitoring, and managing projects more effectively.

03

Site Monitoring

Real-time monitoring of construction sites captures images and video to track progress and identify potential issues — continuous oversight that keeps projects on schedule and within budget.

04

River Mapping

Detailed drone mapping of rivers and surrounding areas provides essential data for construction near water bodies, including river paths, floodplains, and flood risk assessment.

05

Smart City Survey

Comprehensive surveys for smart city projects capture detailed data on infrastructure, traffic patterns, and urban planning elements — valuable insight for efficient, resource-conscious urban design.
